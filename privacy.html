Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nguyen Thanh Hung handles information in Thronebound Whispers (the “Game”).

Information We Collect

The current version of the Game does not require an account, collect names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, photos, or messages. Story progress, unlocked chapters, stars, ending records, and music settings are stored locally on your device using iOS system storage.

Local Game Data

Local save data is used only to provide Game features such as continuing a story, replaying unlocked chapters, recording endings, and remembering audio preferences. Removing the Game or resetting its data may remove this local information. We do not operate a server for these local saves.

Advertising and Rewarded Continuations

The Game may offer an optional rewarded advertisement for a continuation when an advertising provider is configured. In the current build, an unavailable advertisement does not grant a reward and free retry remains available. If a production release enables a third-party advertising provider, that provider may process device, network, advertising, or usage information under its own privacy policy and, where required, after obtaining consent. We will update this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before enabling such a provider in a public release.

Children’s Privacy

The Game is not directed to children in a way that intentionally collects personal information. If a future Game service receives personal information from a child, we will review the matter promptly.

Data Sharing

We do not sell local save data. We do not share personal information because the current version does not collect it. Any future third-party advertising integration will be disclosed as described above.

Security

Local data is handled through iOS-provided storage. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but we limit the current Game data to the local features described in this policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the Game’s features, advertising configuration, or legal requirements change. The updated effective date will appear at the top of this page.
